Why RO comes up so often in Los Lunas

Arsenic occurs naturally in parts of the Middle Rio Grande's groundwater, and the Santa Fe Group aquifer under Los Lunas is no exception. The village recognized that more than a decade ago and installed arsenic treatment at its wells around 2009–2010, and it continues to upgrade the system as the community grows. Its annual Consumer Confidence Report is where you'll find the official numbers.

So why do village customers still ask about RO? Some simply want a second barrier for the water their kids drink. Others want lower dissolved solids for better coffee and clearer ice. And for families on private wells outside the village, nobody treats the water but them, which makes a point-of-use system a practical step once lab results are in.

Village water vs. a private well

Testing
Village homes get our on-site readings for TDS, hardness, pH, chlorine, iron, manganese and sulfur. Well owners get those plus help arranging lab tests for arsenic, nitrate and bacteria.
Where it goes
Under the kitchen sink in both cases, with a dedicated faucet on the counter and a small drain connection.
Pairing
In homes that tested hard, a softener upstream helps the membrane last. On a well, everything whole-house sits after the pressure tank.
Limits
RO isn't a bacteria treatment. If a well tests positive, disinfection comes first, and we'll point you to the right resources.

Reverse osmosis FAQs: Los Lunas

Is Los Lunas village water safe to drink without RO?

The village treats for arsenic and reports its results annually in its Consumer Confidence Report. RO is an optional extra step for families who want lower dissolved solids or an added barrier at the kitchen tap.

Should I get RO if I'm on a well outside the village?

Start with a lab test for arsenic, nitrate and bacteria. If arsenic or nitrate shows up, an under-sink RO is the standard point-of-use approach, and we'll confirm it fits your results.

Does RO waste a lot of water?

Every RO sends some water to the drain. Our tankless design is efficient, and in a place where water matters this much, we size it to how your family actually drinks and cooks.

Can RO connect to my refrigerator?

Often, yes, if the fridge line can be routed from the sink cabinet. We'll look at the layout during your visit.
