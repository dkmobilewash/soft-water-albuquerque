Why hardness shows up on both kinds of Los Ranchos water

Whether your house is on ABCWUA service or pumps from its own well, the water has spent time in contact with valley sediments and aquifer minerals.

🏛️

Utility customers

ABCWUA water blends treated river water with groundwater, and the share of groundwater climbs in dry years. Many village homes see mineral buildup as a result.

🪣

Well owners

An older valley well can carry its own mineral load, sometimes with a little iron. A softener handles hardness and small amounts of iron; it isn't a fix for anything else in the water.

🌵

Desert-dry skin

Low humidity already pulls moisture from skin and hair. Soft water rinses clean of soap film, which many people notice within the first week.

What we check before recommending a softener

The free on-site test measures hardness, TDS, pH, chlorine, iron and manganese, and sulfur. Hardness sets the softener size and the regeneration schedule. Iron tells us whether resin alone can cope or whether the water needs another stage first. Your test results will show exactly where your water lands — we don't guess from a ZIP code.

If the house runs on a private well, we'll also point you toward lab testing for bacteria, nitrate and arsenic. A softener removes none of those, and you deserve to know before spending money on equipment. If the lab finds something, reverse osmosis or another approach may belong in the plan.

Softener FAQs for Los Ranchos

Does a softener make sense if my well water already tastes fine?

Taste and hardness are separate. Well water can taste great and still coat your water heater with scale. The hardness reading during your free test answers the question.

Can my horses drink softened water?

We usually recommend leaving barn and stock-tank lines on untreated water, which also saves salt and resin capacity for the house. We'll map out which lines get softened during the visit.

Will a softener remove arsenic or nitrate from my well?

No. Ion exchange softening targets calcium, magnesium and small amounts of iron. If lab testing turns up arsenic or nitrate, under-sink reverse osmosis is the usual point-of-use approach.

Is there room for a softener in an older home with no garage?

Usually. A utility room, well house or exterior enclosure can work as long as we can reach the main line, a drain and an outlet. We confirm placement before you commit.
