What the free test looks for here

We bring a field kit to your sink and read hardness, TDS, pH, chlorine, iron and manganese, and sulfur on the spot. For an ABCWUA home, the chlorine number usually confirms what your nose already told you. For a well, iron, manganese and sulfur readings decide whether plain carbon is enough or whether specialty media should go in the tank.

A field test can't rule out bacteria or nitrate in a well. If your property sits near pasture, a ditch or an older septic system, we'll help you set up certified lab testing before anything is installed.

Signs a Los Ranchos home could use it

  • The shower smells faintly like a swimming pool
  • Tap water tastes off in coffee or tea
  • Orange or dark staining shows up in a well-fed toilet tank
  • A rotten-egg odor appears when hot water runs
  • Grit collects in faucet aerators after the well pump cycles

Filtration questions from the village

Does ABCWUA use chlorine or chloramine?

ABCWUA disinfects with chlorine. Catalytic carbon is well suited to reducing its taste and smell throughout the house.

My well water smells like sulfur. Will carbon fix it?

Sometimes, but sulfur and iron often call for specialty media. We confirm what's present with the on-site test before choosing what goes in the tank.

Will a whole-home filter make my well water safe to drink?

No filter should be sold that way. Carbon does not remove bacteria, nitrate or arsenic. Lab testing comes first, and drinking-water concerns are usually handled with reverse osmosis at the kitchen sink.

Can one filter serve the main house and a casita?

If both buildings branch off the same line after the meter or pressure tank, often yes. If the casita has its own service, it may need separate equipment. We check during the visit.
