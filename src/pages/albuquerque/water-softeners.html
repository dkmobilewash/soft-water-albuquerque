How hard water shows up across town

Same utility, different symptoms, mostly because the houses are so different.

Nob Hill & University area

Older homes here have had decades of hard water running through them. Owners tend to notice slow-draining aerators, crusty shower valves and water heaters that rumble with sediment.

Westside subdivisions

In Taylor Ranch, Paradise Hills and Ventana Ranch the plumbing is newer, which makes this the cheapest moment to protect it. Tankless heaters in particular don't tolerate scale for long.

Foothills, NE Heights & Four Hills

More bathrooms means more glass to squeegee. Spotted shower doors, cloudy stemware and a white ring on every faucet base are the usual complaints east of the freeway.

South Valley well owner?

A softener can handle hardness and small amounts of iron from a well, but it does nothing for arsenic, nitrate or bacteria. We'll help you arrange lab testing first, and the softener goes after your pressure tank, not before it.

Softener questions from Albuquerque homeowners

Will a softener change how Albuquerque water tastes?

Not much. It swaps hardness minerals for a small amount of sodium. The chlorine taste some people notice comes from ABCWUA's disinfection, and that's a job for carbon filtration, not the softener.

My Nob Hill house has no garage. Where does the softener go?

Usually near where the main line enters: a utility closet, a laundry area or a protected spot outside. We look at the space during the test and tell you exactly where it would sit.

Is it worth softening a brand-new Westside home?

That's actually the best time. New fixtures, a new water heater and especially a tankless unit stay cleaner when scale never gets a foothold.

Why does my hardness seem to change year to year?

The Water Authority's blend shifts toward groundwater in drought years. A metered softener simply regenerates based on the water you use, and we can adjust the hardness setting if your results change.
