How a water softener works

Hard water is loaded with dissolved calcium and magnesium. As water passes through your softener, a bed of resin beads captures those minerals through a process called ion exchange. When the resin is full, the system automatically regenerates with a brine rinse and flushes the minerals down the drain.

Our digital metered valves only regenerate when you've actually used enough water — saving salt and water compared to older timer-based units.
