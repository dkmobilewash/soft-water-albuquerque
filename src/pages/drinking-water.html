Why reverse osmosis?

Reverse osmosis pushes water through an ultra-fine membrane that reduces a wide range of dissolved solids and contaminants — far more than a pitcher or fridge filter can. The result is clean, great-tasting water for drinking, coffee, cooking and ice.

  • Reduces total dissolved solids (TDS), lead, arsenic, fluoride, nitrates and more*
  • Tankless 600 GPD design — fast flow, compact under-sink footprint
  • Alkaline remineralization stage for a smooth, balanced taste
  • Smart filter-life indicator
  • Dedicated designer faucet included

*Reduction levels vary by contaminant and water conditions. We'll review your water test results with you.
