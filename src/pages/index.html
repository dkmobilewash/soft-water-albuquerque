The signs of hard water in your Albuquerque home

Much of the Albuquerque metro's water tests hard to very hard. Those dissolved minerals leave their mark everywhere water touches.

🚿

White crust on fixtures

Chalky scale builds up on faucets, showerheads and glass doors no matter how often you scrub.

🍽️

Spotty dishes

Cloudy glasses and film on dishes straight out of the dishwasher.

🔥

Short-lived water heaters

Scale coats heating elements, cutting efficiency and shortening the life of your heater and appliances.

🧴

Dry skin & dull hair

In our dry desert climate, hard water makes itchy skin and brittle hair even worse.

👕

Stiff, faded laundry

Minerals trap soap in fabric, leaving clothes scratchy and colors dull.

🧽

Soap that won't lather

You use more soap, shampoo and detergent — and still don't feel clean.
