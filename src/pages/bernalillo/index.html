Town of Bernalillo · Sandoval County

Bernalillo's water, explained before anyone tries to sell you anything

The town treats its own well water. Knowing what that treatment already handles makes it much easier to see what, if anything, your home still needs.

The short version

Who supplies it
The Town of Bernalillo runs its own water utility. It is not part of the Albuquerque system or the Rio Rancho system.
Where it comes from
Groundwater, pumped from the town's wells along the Rio Grande valley.
How it's treated
According to the town's water quality report, a pressure-filter system using ferric chloride removes naturally occurring arsenic, and sodium hypochlorite disinfects the water.
Backup supply
An emergency connection ties into Rio Rancho's system.
Official numbers
Published each year in the town's Consumer Confidence Report.

What the town's treatment covers, and what it doesn't

Arsenic is a natural part of the geology in much of the Middle Rio Grande region, and regulated systems are required to treat it to the federal limit. Bernalillo handles that at the plant with iron-based pressure filters, which is a serious piece of infrastructure for a town this size. The hypochlorite step then keeps the water disinfected on its way through the mains, from the houses along Camino del Pueblo out to the newer subdivisions.

That process is built to remove arsenic and disinfect. It isn't designed to soften. Calcium and magnesium dissolved from the aquifer can pass right through, and so can the disinfectant residual that's supposed to be there. Those are the two things Bernalillo homeowners tend to notice day to day: mineral buildup on fixtures and appliances, and the faint pool-like smell of chlorine in a hot shower.

Neither is a health problem. Both are fixable. A water softener in Bernalillo handles the minerals, whole-home carbon filtration takes the edge off the chlorine taste and odor, and a reverse osmosis system under the kitchen sink is for people who want their drinking water as low in dissolved solids as they can get it.

Outside town limits?

Some properties around Bernalillo aren't on the town system. If you're on a private well, nobody treats it but you. We'll do the on-site test and help you get lab testing for bacteria, nitrate and arsenic before talking about equipment.

Bernalillo water FAQs

Does the Town of Bernalillo treat its water for arsenic?

Yes. The town's water quality report describes a pressure-filter system using ferric chloride to remove naturally occurring arsenic, with sodium hypochlorite for disinfection. Check the town's annual report for current figures.

If the town already treats my water, why would I want a softener?

Arsenic treatment and disinfection aren't softening. Hardness minerals can still reach your tap, and those are what cause scale. Our free test shows whether that's the case at your address.

Is Bernalillo water the same as Rio Rancho water?

No. They're separate utilities with separate wells and separate treatment. The two systems have an emergency connection, but day to day Bernalillo homes get Bernalillo's water.
