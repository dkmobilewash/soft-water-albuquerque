Water softeners · Bernalillo

Arsenic treatment isn't softening. Here's the difference.

Bernalillo's town utility runs its well water through ferric chloride pressure filters to remove naturally occurring arsenic, then disinfects it. That's an important job. Pulling out calcium and magnesium just isn't part of it.

So the minerals that leave scale on faucets and inside water heaters can still arrive at your house. A softener is the piece of equipment made to deal with them. Our free test shows whether your Bernalillo address actually needs one.

What a softener won't do for Bernalillo water

We'd rather say this plainly than have you find out later. An ion-exchange softener doesn't remove arsenic, nitrate, chlorine or bacteria. On town water, arsenic is the utility's job, and its annual water quality report is where you'll find the official results. If you'd like an extra layer at the kitchen sink anyway, that's what reverse osmosis is for. If the chlorine smell from the town's hypochlorite disinfection bothers you, carbon filtration is the fix.

A softener can handle small amounts of iron. That rarely matters on town water, but it can matter if you're outside town limits on your own well.

Bernalillo softener FAQs

Doesn't the town's arsenic treatment already soften the water?

No. The pressure-filter process is designed to remove arsenic, and the hypochlorite step disinfects. Neither is built to remove calcium and magnesium, which is what softening means.

Will a softener remove arsenic from my Bernalillo water?

No. Softeners remove hardness. For arsenic, the town treats at the source, and reverse osmosis is the usual point-of-use option if you want additional treatment at the kitchen sink.

Can you fit a softener in an older Bernalillo home without a garage?

Usually, yes. We look for a spot on the main line with a drain nearby, often a utility room or closet, and we'll tell you exactly what's involved during the test visit.

How often will I add salt?

It depends on your hardness and how much water you use. The metered valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt use reasonable.
